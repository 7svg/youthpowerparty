FILE 01
Led by Herat

Stop Paper Leaks

Securing the exam pipeline so every result reflects merit — not who saw the paper first.

Read full speech

Friends, students, and the youth of India.

We are told from childhood: study hard, sacrifice your sleep, and your future will be secure. Our parents empty their savings to pay for your coaching fees and exam forms. But what is the reality today? On the morning of your exam, after years of sweat and stress, you discover that the paper you prepared for was sold on WhatsApp or Telegram the night before.

This is not a system failure. It is a crime. It is the direct theft of your time, your family's money, and your future.

The corrupt officials and the paper leakers have turned our education system into a black market. But I stand before you today as your candidate for the Youth Power Party with one unbreakable promise:

Under my watch, I will not let a single paper leak. Not a single one.

We cannot fight a modern mafia with outdated methods. This is my three-step action plan to lock the system down forever:

First, Digital Armor: We will end the physical transport of papers in trucks. Question papers will be sent via end-to-end encrypted networks directly to exam centers. The password to open them will only generate 15 minutes before the exam, and papers will be printed securely inside the hall.

Second, Crushing the Mafia: We will enforce zero-bail laws for anyone involved in a leak. Whether it is a low-level clerk or a billionaire coaching center owner, they will face up to ten years in prison and a minimum fine of one crore rupees. We will seize their properties immediately.

Third, Cyber Checkpoint: We will deploy an independent cybersecurity task force and AI monitoring to track every digital footprint, stopping syndicates before they even try.

The Youth Power Party believes,

Merit should never be defeated by money. When you sit for an exam, the only thing that should matter is your preparation. Give me your vote, and together we will crush this corrupt network. We will protect your hard work, and we will take back our future!

Youth Power Party — because India's future deserves a fair chance.

Thank you, and Jai Hind!

FILE 03
Led by Moksh

AI & Digital Balance

Using technology to expand what we learn — without letting screens control our lives.

Read full speech

Good morning, respected teachers and my dear friends.

Today I stand before you to speak on a topic that touches every second of our daily lives: the impact of technology on youth. Technology has made this generation the most connected, informed, and capable in human history. But like two sides of a coin, it brings both opportunities and challenges.

On the positive side, technology empowers us in remarkable ways. Learning is no longer restricted to classrooms — high-quality lectures and research tools sit at our fingertips, 24/7. Social media lets us collaborate and build friendships across continents. From coding and digital art to video editing, technology gives us tools to build career-ready skills early, while smart apps simplify daily tasks.

On the other hand, we can't ignore the pitfalls of excessive use. Digital addiction and high screen time reduce attention spans and foster procrastination. Prolonged device use brings sedentary habits, poor sleep, eye strain, and isolation. Social comparison online fuels anxiety, and unfiltered access exposes young users to scams and cyberbullying. Over-reliance on virtual interaction can even weaken our face-to-face communication.

In conclusion, technology itself is neither good nor bad — it's a tool. The real power lies in how we choose to use it. As the youth of today and leaders of tomorrow, our goal should be digital balance: using technology to expand our horizons, without letting it control our lives. Thank you.

FILE 04
Led by Harsh

eSports for All

Equipment, tournaments, and prize money — so talent matters more than money.

Read full speech

Respected teachers and my dear friends, good morning!

I'm Harsh, VP candidate from the Development and Improvement team. Real development means equal opportunity for every student — rich or poor. Our project is Gaming and eSports for All.

Gaming today isn't just fun — it's a career. But many talented students can't play because they don't have phones, PCs, or money for tournaments. That's not fair. Talent shouldn't depend on money.

So our plan is simple. A Gaming Support Bank: we'll collect keyboards, mice, and headphones, and bring in sponsors, so any student can borrow equipment free for tournaments. A School eSports League: two tournaments a year, on games that run on mobile and school PCs. Prize money — ₹10,000, ₹8,000, ₹5,000 for the top finishers, funded by sponsors, not by you, to use for studies or home. And Growth with Gaming: we'll also teach time management and digital safety alongside it.

This is about equality, opportunity, and the future of our school. I'm asking for your vote so that the student in the last row who thinks "I can't afford this" can one day say "I won." Let's build a school where talent matters more than money. Let's press start on development. Thank you!
